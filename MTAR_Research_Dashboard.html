Business Snapshot

MTAR Technologies (est. 1969, Hyderabad) is India's premier precision engineering company manufacturing mission-critical components for nuclear power, space/aerospace, defence, and clean energy. Products include SOFC hot boxes (Bloom Energy), cryogenic engine turbopumps (ISRO), nuclear coolant channel assemblies (NPCIL), and defence components (DRDO/Rafael/GKN). 7 manufacturing facilities across Hyderabad in a 4km radius, plus a dedicated export facility. [Company website, AR FY25]

Its moat is sole-source supplier status in mission-critical components — MTAR is the only Indian manufacturer of SOFC hot boxes for Bloom Energy, nuclear coolant channels for NPCIL, and several ISRO propulsion components. Requalification timelines of 18–36 months make switching nearly impossible. This concentrated sole-source status is both greatest strength and greatest vulnerability. [MOSL Research]

The transformative event: Bloom Energy's AI data-centre pivot. Bloom signed a $5B AI infrastructure partnership with Brookfield and engaged Oracle/hyperscalers for 24/7 uninterrupted SOFC power. Bloom FY2026 revenue guidance of $3.1–3.3B (+55% YoY) directly drives MTAR's hot-box order volume. MTAR's capacity expansion 8K→12K→20K→30K units tracks Bloom's manufacturing doubling exactly. [Bloom Q4'25 8-K Feb 2026]

Risk profile: Extreme Bloom concentration (~60% revenues), historical CFO/PAT divergence (FY23 CFO negative despite ₹103 Cr PAT), and 227× TTM P/E at ₹4,900 — zero margin for error. [Analyst inference]
